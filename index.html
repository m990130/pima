About Me

Hi, I'm Pingchuan 🐴.

I am a final-year MCML PhD student in the Computer Vision & Learning Group at the University of Munich, under the supervision of Prof. Björn Ommer.

My research sits at the intersection of representation learning and generative AI. I work on multimodal representation learning, especially alignment and distributional relationships in vision-language models; generative modeling, including diffusion and flow-based models; and generative model priors beyond synthesis, where the general visual knowledge learned by large generative models is used for tasks such as depth estimation, 3D modeling, correspondence, and studying how artistic style evolves over time.

Earlier in my research, I worked on deep metric learning and style transfer, studying robust visual representations and how style can be defined and edited from a computer vision perspective.

🤓 As I am approaching to the end of my PhD (Aug / Sep 2026), I am currently looking for internships or full-time positions as a Research Scientist, Applied Scientist, or ML / AI Research Engineer (preferably Europe).
